# Sammati DPDP Readiness

Check how ready your organisation is for India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, from inside a conversation with Claude. It is the same assessment as the free self-check at [sammati.io/assessment](https://sammati.io/assessment).

## Use it

Ask Claude to run a DPDP readiness check. You can choose a short Pulse Check or the complete assessment. If you paste your privacy notice or consent screen text, Claude reads it first and fills in the answers it can, then asks only about what is left. You get a score, your biggest gaps, and a prioritised list of next steps.

The deadlines worth knowing: the Consent Manager framework opens on 13 November 2026, and full compliance is due on 13 May 2027.

## Data

The assessment runs inside your Claude conversation, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to have the report emailed to you.

To get the report by email, the plugin's **Sammati** connector must be connected. In Claude Code it connects automatically. In claude.ai, Claude Desktop and Cowork, open the plugin's **Connectors** tab and click Connect once; there is no sign-in and no URL to enter.

If you do, Claude first shows you Sammati's privacy notice and asks for your agreement. It then sends your email address, your name and organisation if you give them, and your answer to each question (not your free-text descriptions and never a pasted document) to the Sammati connector at sammati.io, run by Sammati (Arborworld India Private Limited). The score is worked out there and a PDF report is emailed to you. Your agreement is recorded in Sammati's consent ledger. Separately, and only if you say yes, a Sammati specialist may contact you about closing your gaps: you will get one email with a button to confirm, and Sammati's team is not notified about you until you press it. You can say no to that and still get the report, and you can withdraw your consent at any time by replying to the email.

## Not legal advice

The result is a gap analysis to help you plan. It is not legal advice and not a certification of compliance. Speak to a qualified lawyer for advice on your situation.
